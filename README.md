Frame Privacy is made by Frame Labs Inc., a Canadian company based in Alberta.

- **Data brokers.** Frame looks for your name, address and phone on people-search sites and guides each opt-out across 1,228 data brokers in Canada, the US and Europe.
- **Breaches.** Verified emails are checked against known breaches, with what leaked and what to change first.
- **Re-listings.** Brokers often list people again. Frame tracks every opt-out and reminds you when to check again.

## Open source

- [data-brokers-list](https://github.com/cvs0/data-brokers-list): the open data-broker catalog, with opt-out pages mapped
- [frame-extension](https://github.com/frameprivacy/frame-extension): the Frame browser extension, free with or without an account
- [column-seal](https://github.com/frameprivacy/column-seal): versioned AES-256-GCM envelopes, key rotation and HMAC blind indexes for database columns in Node.js
- [ratelimit-stores](https://github.com/frameprivacy/ratelimit-stores): atomic fixed-window rate limiting and single-holder leases on Postgres, Upstash Redis or memory
- [parity-i18n](https://github.com/frameprivacy/parity-i18n): type-safe i18n where every translation must match the source's shape, plus correct French typography
